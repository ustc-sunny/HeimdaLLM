# Results handoff — partial evidence, not a completed Stage 1 result

On a fixed 128-candidate public Qwen pool, 64 guidance slots, two AG News source clients, and the 512-example development split, three archived, non-private FL seed-57 runs completed 50 FedFwd updates under the same recorded downstream flags. Their final development accuracies were 75.00% for private-vote synthetic guidance, 70.31% for deterministic public-random guidance, and 72.07% for matched same-source real guidance. The private-vote minus public-random difference was +4.6875 percentage points on this single seed. Archive checksums, completion flags, 51 evaluation points, and public-random selection replay were verified locally, while live source files and the exact update operator still require server-side confirmation. These values are descriptive pilot evidence, not a replicated comparison or a privacy result.

The public NLI selection contains 64 candidates from the same pool. Its 59/64 auxiliary label agreement is a classifier proxy; **downstream accuracy is TBD**. The three planned ε=4 private DP releases and their downstream outcomes are **TBD**. No release-level or end-to-end FL privacy claim is supported by this handoff. The official test set was not used in these archives. Do not insert the provisional numbers into a formal multi-seed/DP results table until the protocol audit and missing runs are completed.
